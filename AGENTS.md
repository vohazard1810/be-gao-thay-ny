# Character Bible & Visual Guidelines (Bé Gạo & Thầy Ny Universe)

## 1. Character Identity References (STRICT)

### Primary Characters
- **Bé Gạo**:
  - **Species & Age**: Vietnamese human toddler girl, approximately 3 years old.
  - **Head & Face**: Round toddler cheeks, cute button nose, expressive dark eyes, chin-length dark bob haircut with bangs, exactly one yellow flower hair clip on the side.
  - **Body Proportions**: Short arms and legs, chubby toddler build, small body proportions (NEVER make her older, taller, or slimmer).
  - **Outfit**: Cream short-sleeved undershirt, sage-green pinafore dress with exactly two yellow buttons on the straps, mustard-yellow ankle socks, cream walking shoes, and one yellow smiling star crossbody bag.

- **Thỏ Bông**:
  - **Species & Size**: Plush-like white bunny with long floppy ears, pink inner ears, fluffy white tail. Height reaches approximately Bé Gạo's waist (distinctly smaller than Bé Gạo).
  - **Permanent Outfit**: Lilac/lavender knitted sleeveless sweater vest.
  - **Story Prop**: Blue knitted scarf (removable story prop in the scarf-finding adventure; NOT part of the permanent daily outfit).

### Supporting Teachers
- **Thầy Ny**:
  - **Identity**: Young adult Vietnamese male teacher, gentle and friendly demeanor.
  - **Appearance**: Wavy dark brown hair, warm smiling face, mustard-yellow knitted cardigan sweater, slate-blue collared shirt with a round teal chest badge, cream/beige trousers, brown leather oxford shoes.

- **Cô Hươu**:
  - **Identity**: Gentle anthropomorphic fawn/deer teacher.
  - **Appearance**: Light brown spotted fawn fur, small deer antlers, soft fawn ears with white inner fluff, rosy cheeks, wearing a dusty-rose / coral-pink pinafore dress with white puffy collar and sleeves, small green clover leaf brooch, brown flat shoes.

### Supporting Children (Classmates & Friends)
- **Sóc Nâu** (Top-Left):
  - **Species**: Little brown squirrel with tufted ears, warm amber-brown fur, fluffy curled tail.
  - **Outfit**: Cream t-shirt, sage-green overalls with an acorn emblem/badge on the chest.

- **Mèo Mây** (Top-Right):
  - **Species**: Soft silver-gray tabby kitten with delicate gray stripes and pink cheeks.
  - **Outfit**: Dusty-pink pinafore dress with white collar and sleeves, pale green bow hair clip next to her right ear.

- **Cún Đốm** (Bottom-Left):
  - **Species**: Cheerful cream-and-tan puppy with a distinct brown patch over his left eye and floppy brown ears.
  - **Outfit**: Light blue overalls with golden shoulder straps over a yellow/cream t-shirt.

- **Vịt Mơ** (Bottom-Right):
  - **Species**: Cute fluffy yellow duckling with small feather crest on head, bright orange beak, warm dark eyes.

## 2. Art Style & Illustration Directives (MANDATORY)

- **Aesthetic**: Soft 2D hand-painted picture-book style with subtle watercolor/gouache textures, warm paper texture backgrounds, delicate organic pencil outlines, and pastel earthy palette.
- **Strict Prohibitions**:
  - NO 3D CGI or glossy rendering.
  - NO flat vector clipart or sterile geometric Canvas drawings.
  - NO emoji-style faces.
  - NO photorealism or hyper-realistic textures.
  - NO redesigning of approved characters or unrequested costume changes.
- **Scene Composition Rule**: Include ONLY the characters explicitly named in the specific scene script. Do NOT crowd every scene with all supporting characters.
